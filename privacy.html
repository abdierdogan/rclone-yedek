Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

rclone-yedek is a personal backup tool used only by its owner.

  • It requests the Google Drive scope drive.file, which only allows access to files the tool itself creates.
  • It uploads the owner's files from the owner's computer to the owner's Google Drive. Nothing else is collected.
  • No data is sent to, stored on or shared with any server or third party other than Google Drive.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
